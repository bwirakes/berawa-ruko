# BERAWA 25 — 30-Year Land Lease Plan

Prepared 18 July 2026 for the 25-are (2,500 sqm) holding at 1053 Jalan Pantai Berawa, beside Mosto.

## Executive decision

Market the property first as a **whole-site, 30-year commercial ground lease** to one well-capitalised operator or development partner. This best matches the owner's goal of renting the land, limits owner capex, and avoids the access, infrastructure, vacancy and management risk of creating many small plots.

Run a two-branch price strategy only after the site facts are verified:

| Verified condition | Launch/test ask | Negotiation target | 30-year target value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Direct Jalan Pantai Berawa frontage + Pink/tourism or equivalent commercial use | IDR 68m/are/year | IDR 60–65m/are/year | IDR 45.0–48.75b |
| Rear/side access through the shops or Yellow/residential constraints | IDR 55m/are/year | IDR 48–53m/are/year | IDR 36.0–39.75b |

Do not publish the asking price until title, zoning, access, frontage, tax basis and usable area are confirmed. Use “guide available in the investor brief” publicly. Do not adopt the IDR 72m premium comparable as the default anchor: it is roughly 37% above the fresh core-comp median and is an asking price, not a closed transaction.

This is a marketing strategy, not an appraisal. Commission a local MAPPI valuer and obtain evidence of achieved rents before setting a binding reserve.

## Offer architecture

### Recommended offer: whole-site master lease

- 30-year notarial lease over the whole verified 2,500 sqm.
- Tenant funds design, permits, construction, operating fit-out and ongoing maintenance.
- Owner provides clean title, warranties, legal access and agreed utility connection points.
- Public guide is withheld; qualified parties receive a gross-price heads of terms.
- Ask for evidence of funds, beneficial ownership, relevant operating/development track record and a concept note before releasing the complete data room.
- Require a meaningful exclusivity deposit after heads of terms, with an agreed diligence timetable and clearly defined refund conditions.

### Payment structures to test

1. **Upfront consideration — preferred.** Full amount at execution or in a short signing/hand-over schedule. This minimises credit and inflation risk.
2. **Two-stage close.** A substantial signing payment followed by the balance at verified vacant possession/permit milestone, secured by a bank guarantee or equivalent.
3. **Five-year tranches — only for exceptional covenant.** Price the financing and default risk explicitly; index later tranches and secure them. Compare the present value against an upfront bid rather than comparing nominal totals.

Indonesian tax guidance states that Article 4(2) final tax on gross land/building rent is 10%, with withholding mechanics depending on the tenant. Clearly state whether every quoted number is **gross contract consideration** or **owner-net proceeds**. For illustration only, an owner-net IDR 60m rate would require IDR 66.67m gross if a full 10% withholding applies. Confirm the actual treatment with Indonesian tax counsel and the notary. Source: [Directorate General of Taxes](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/node/80372).

### Heads of terms checklist

- Exact parties, beneficial owners and authority to contract.
- Cadastral/title details, surveyed usable area and boundary plan.
- Commencement, 30-year expiry and conditions precedent.
- Gross rent, tax/withholding allocation, currency and payment timing.
- Permitted uses and obligation to obtain KBLI/NIB, environmental, PBG and SLF approvals.
- Access width, turning rights, parking, loading, ride-hailing and construction access.
- Water, power, wastewater, drainage, waste, telecom and upgrade responsibility.
- Sublease, assignment, change of control and financing/security rights.
- Construction approval process and treatment of improvements at expiry.
- Insurance, indemnities, nuisance/noise, maintenance and compliance.
- Default, cure, termination, force majeure and dispute forum.
- Binding effect on successors/heirs and title-transfer protections.
- Extension option with a precise formula; avoid “market price by mutual agreement” without a deadlock mechanism.

## Target tenants and partners

### Tier 1 — direct first

1. Bali/Indonesia lifestyle-property developers with completed commercial compounds.
2. Regional food-hall, gourmet-market or multi-brand hospitality operators.
3. Premium clinical wellness, diagnostics, recovery or aesthetics platforms.
4. International/respected Indonesian retail groups seeking a Bali flagship cluster.
5. Family education, childcare or enrichment operators with proven multi-site economics.

### Tier 2 — selective

- Hotel and villa groups only if the scheme has credible demand evidence and a strong covenant.
- Interior, design, villa-supply and mobility showroom operators.
- Institutional family offices or operating partners seeking a Bali platform site.

### Avoid as the lead thesis

- Generic coworking.
- Another undifferentiated gym/sauna/cold-plunge or padel concept.
- Commodity convenience retail.
- A speculative single boutique hotel/villa project based only on headline arrivals.
- A single 2,500 sqm operating tenant without strong covenant, guarantees and replacement flexibility.

The best end-use concept is a curated day-to-night compound: one 700–1,000 sqm anchor, three to five 200–400 sqm destination pads, a few 80–150 sqm services, shared landscape, safe pickup, real parking and separated loading. This is a tenant/developer concept, not a commitment by the owner to build it.

## Marketing plan

### Phase 0 — make the asset marketable (weeks 1–3)

- Commission boundary/topographic survey and reconcile the 1053/Mosto address pins.
- Obtain current title copy, land-tax evidence, zoning/ITR/KKPR position and owner authority documents.
- Verify whether the land itself has direct road frontage or relies on access through/beside the existing shops.
- Complete drainage/flood, power, water, wastewater and traffic/access notes.
- Run a seven-day movement study: pedestrian, scooter and vehicle counts in 30-minute blocks; weekday/weekend; rain/dry; direction, queueing, ride-hailing and loading conflicts.
- Obtain three broker opinions, a MAPPI valuation and anonymous confirmation calls on the closest 72m, 62.5m, 55m and 43m listings.
- Prepare the owner-approved price reserve and concession ladder.

### Phase 1 — private launch (weeks 4–7)

- Build a 60-account target list across Bali, Jakarta, Singapore and Australia.
- Send personalised outreach to decision-makers, not generic property blasts.
- Appoint no more than three credible commercial brokers under consistent written terms and a single source of truth.
- Give each lead a tracking code and require weekly pipeline reporting.
- Hold two small site breakfasts/walks for operators, developers, architects and tenant representatives.
- Install one premium street sign with a QR code to the qualification page.

### Phase 2 — controlled public campaign (weeks 8–12)

- Launch the land page on `berawashops.com/land` in English and Indonesian.
- Run Google Search campaigns around commercial land lease / long lease / Berawa development terms.
- Use LinkedIn for developer, expansion, real-estate and hospitality decision-makers.
- Use Instagram/Meta for visual retargeting and lookalike reach, but optimise to qualified brief requests rather than cheap form fills.
- Publish one architect massing study, the measured traffic findings and a “why this address” evidence carousel.
- Syndicate a consistent listing to selected portals; do not allow brokers to invent different zoning, size or prices.

### Phase 3 — conversion (weeks 10–16)

- Weekly 20-minute bid/pipeline review.
- Score prospects on funds, covenant, concept fit, decision speed, permit credibility and price.
- Site walk → NDA/data-room access → concept interview → heads of terms → deposit/exclusivity → diligence → notarial lease.
- Create competitive tension with a real bid deadline, but only when two or more qualified parties are active.
- If no credible whole-site proposal by day 120, compare: revised price, longer marketing period, two large parcels, or owner-enabled infrastructure. Do not jump straight to many small plots.

### Initial budget envelope

Set aside approximately IDR 75–150m for the 90-day launch, excluding appraisal, survey/legal diligence, broker success fees and any physical works. Existing photography, brand assets, brochure and the completed building reduce creative costs. Agree every broker success fee in writing and compare at least three proposals rather than assuming a market percentage.

## Website strategy

### Recommended public structure

Use **one public brand and domain**: add `/land` to `berawashops.com`. The existing site, photography, bilingual setup, contact details and built-property credibility make this faster and more trustworthy than starting over.

The page should do one job: turn a credible operator/developer into a qualified brief request.

1. Hero: “25 are / 30 years” with the address and an explicit land-lease label.
2. Verified site facts: survey area, frontage/access, zoning, utilities and availability.
3. Parcel/boundary plan and optional architect massing—not generic Bali stock imagery.
4. Demand evidence: measured traffic and verifiable anchors, with dates and sources.
5. Target-use thesis and the whole-site offer.
6. Adjacent BERAWA 1053 proof, clearly labelled so it is not mistaken for an improvement on the land.
7. Qualification form: intended use, company, track record, funding, opening and decision timeline.
8. Data-room CTA and WhatsApp fallback.

Do not claim “high foot traffic” or publish unverified distances. Replace both with measured site evidence.

### Should there be another website?

**Not another public marketing website yet.** Two public domains would split search authority, create content drift and let brokers circulate conflicting facts.

Create a separate **private deal room** after NDA instead. It should contain title, survey, zoning, access, utility, tax, draft terms, Q&A and version-controlled downloads. If a memorable campaign domain such as `berawa25.com` is acquired, use it only as a redirect to `berawashops.com/land`, not as an independent content site.

## Due diligence gate before launch

No price or permitted-use claim should be final until the following are complete:

- Title/authenticity, encumbrances, disputes, owner identity, spouse/heir/company approvals.
- Exact land boundary, measured net area, setbacks and any public/right-of-way impact.
- Current spatial plan and site-specific commercial/tourism permissions.
- Legal and physical access for customers, construction, loading and emergencies.
- Flood/drainage and wastewater solution.
- Power/water/telecom capacity and cost to upgrade.
- Neighbour/noise restrictions and operating-hour constraints.
- Tax/withholding, VAT if relevant, notary and transaction costs.
- Existing occupancies, informal uses and vacant-possession timing.
- Foreign-investment/permitted-business structure for the selected tenant.

BKPM strengthened Bali investment oversight in 2026 and highlighted violations involving environmental approvals, PBG, SLF and activities closed to foreign investment. Compliance is therefore a core sales asset, not paperwork to postpone. Source: [BKPM, 22 January 2026](https://bkpm.go.id/index.php/id/info/siaran-pers/perkuat-pengawasan-investasi-melalui-pembentukan-desk-investasi).
