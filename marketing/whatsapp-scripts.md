# BERAWA 1053 - WhatsApp Reply Scripts

Copy-paste templates for handling leasing enquiries fast. Use WhatsApp Business (free) so you get greeting/away auto-replies, Quick Replies, and Labels.

Replace `[FROM PRICE]` with your published rate before using the pricing script.

---

## Setup (one time, WhatsApp Business)

- **Greeting message**: auto-sends to anyone who messages for the first time.
- **Away message**: auto-sends outside your set hours.
- **Quick Replies**: save each script below under a shortcut (e.g. `/price`, `/viewing`, `/units`) so you fire them in two taps.
- **Labels**: tag every chat as `New lead`, `Viewing booked`, `Negotiating`, `Won`, `Lost`. This is your free CRM.

## 1. Greeting (auto-reply, first message)

**EN**
> Thanks for your interest in BERAWA 1053. You are speaking with the leasing team. To help fast, could you share: (1) your business or concept, (2) which unit (90 / 100 / 135 sqm or unsure), and (3) your ideal move-in date? We reply quickly, 7 days a week.

**ID**
> Terima kasih atas minat Anda pada BERAWA 1053. Anda terhubung dengan tim leasing. Agar cepat kami bantu, boleh info: (1) bisnis atau konsep Anda, (2) unit yang diminati (90 / 100 / 135 m² atau belum yakin), dan (3) target tanggal masuk? Kami balas cepat, 7 hari seminggu.

## 4. Qualifying questions (ask 2-3, not all at once)

**EN**
> A few quick questions so I can match you to the right space:
> - What is the concept (retail, F&B, wellness, office, other)?
> - Which size fits, or should I recommend one?
> - Target move-in date?
> - Lease length you have in mind?
**ID**
> Beberapa pertanyaan singkat agar saya cocokkan dengan ruang yang tepat:
> - Konsepnya apa (retail, F&B, wellness, kantor, lainnya)?
> - Ukuran yang cocok, atau perlu saya rekomendasikan?
> - Target tanggal masuk?
> - Durasi sewa yang diinginkan?

## 6. Book the viewing (always push to this)

**EN**
> The best way to feel the space is in person. I have slots [day] and [day] this week. Which works? I will send the pin and meet you there. Viewings take about 20 minutes.
**ID**
> Cara terbaik merasakan ruangnya adalah datang langsung. Saya ada slot [hari] dan [hari] minggu ini. Mana yang cocok? Saya kirim pin lokasi dan temui Anda di sana. Kunjungan sekitar 20 menit.

## 7. Follow-ups (no reply)

**Same day, no reply**
> EN: Just making sure you got my note. Want me to hold a viewing slot for you this week?
> ID: Sekadar memastikan pesan saya sampai. Mau saya pegangkan slot kunjungan minggu ini?

**3 days later**
> EN: Still keen on BERAWA 1053? We are getting steady interest and only have three units. Happy to answer anything or get you in for a look.
> ID: Masih tertarik dengan BERAWA 1053? Peminatnya cukup ramai dan hanya ada tiga unit. Saya siap bantu pertanyaan atau jadwalkan kunjungan.

**After a viewing**
> EN: Great seeing you at the space. What did you think? Happy to walk through terms whenever you are ready. I can hold the unit briefly while you decide.
> ID: Senang bertemu di lokasi. Bagaimana menurut Anda? Saya siap bahas detail sewa kapan pun Anda siap. Saya bisa tahan unit sebentar selama Anda memutuskan.

## 8. Scarcity / close (use honestly)

**EN**
> Quick heads up: we have another viewing on the [unit] this week. If it is the one for you, let me know and I will prioritise your application.
**ID**
> Info singkat: ada kunjungan lain untuk unit [unit] minggu ini. Kalau ini unit yang Anda cari, kabari saya dan akan saya prioritaskan aplikasi Anda.

---

### Speed wins
- Reply within minutes during the day. Most leases go to whoever responds first and books the viewing.
- Always end every message with a question or a next step.
- Push to a viewing as early as politely possible. In-person closes.
